allocations, balanced journal, idempotency result and audit together."

RECORDING RECOVERY NOTES — NOT NARRATION
- Do not disburse the same loan twice; a duplicate must conflict. Start a fresh application
  for another take. Do not delete existing borrowers, loans, journals or database volumes.
- A network timeout is an unknown outcome. Inspect records and retry the exact frozen
  request. Never silently generate a fresh key to retry a possibly committed payment.
- At 1024px or mobile, use the inspector's internal scrolling/bottom sheet. The supplied
  desktop framing makes money, context and entries visible together.
- If the first due date, product or business date differs, the stated schedule/interest
  figures may no longer apply. Recreate the intended fresh example, not fabricated output.
- Audit History scans at most 5,000 global records. The recommended fresh recording DB
  keeps this loan's events visible. The standalone Activity view offers real pagination.
- Services already running from other work should not be killed indiscriminately. Stop
  only your labelled server Terminal, or coordinate alternate ports before recording.
- Rehearse and record the actual screen. No screenshot or video capture is claimed merely
  because this script exists. The separate verification report states what was executed.
